      The problem

      Models freeze the world on the day they were trained. The world keeps moving.

      Prices

      Plans and fees change monthly. A month-old price is already a guess.

      Software versions

      Releases and LTS lines turn over every quarter.

      Company facts

      Leaders, products and policies drift over months.

      Laws & rules

      Slow to change — and costly when an answer misses it.

      Method

      From a confident answer
      to a current one.

      Five steps, every one of them traceable: each search, each model call and each quote is stored and checked against the source text.

      1. 01

        Split & flag

        The answer becomes atomic claims, each flagged by how fast it expires — prices in about a month, versions in a quarter, company facts in six months, rules in a year.

      2. 02

        Search live

        Tavily searches official sites, the news (Brazilian outlets for Portuguese claims) and scientific articles. Diverse types, never two from one site, blogs last.

      3. 03

        Read together

        One model call through Bifrost reads every source for a claim, knowing today’s date. Every quote it returns is matched against the page.

      4. 04

        Weigh & score

        Copies count once; official and scientific sources weigh most. Freshness = how recent the confirming sources are × how many agree.

      5. 05

        Rewrite

        A last call writes the answer again from verified quotes only, with numbered citations, next to what the AI said.
